# Required declaration relationships and source evidence

Required declaration relationship inspection is an explicit addition to source or effective inspection. It resolves established `sdfRequired` references, all directly contained affordance/grouping classes sharing a Given Name, and carrying `true` markers under RFC 9880 Section 4.5; pointer targets require a declaration position under Section 1.2.3, while standalone carrier markers do not instantiate definitions. Relationship completion remains separate from validity and target capability. Outside authored identities have source evidence and capability `not_requested`; callers request their effective content separately. Copied pointers relocate by coordinate under ADR 0036: Copied reference relocation. Data-only applicability remains a needed, scoped limitation rather than guessed optionality, following ADR 0006: Scoped reference failures and ADR 0023: Supplied global composition and dependency scope.

Effective nodes retain their initial Declaration contribution before ancestor refinements or content donor substitution. Surviving coordinates retain it; genuinely new or reinserted members use their introducing contribution. Source position is independently established before exposing a source Definition. Current effective coordinates, nearest content origins and importing consumer remain separate, including alternative branch copies, following ADR 0020: Owned effective views and nearest origins and ADR 0024: Effective alternatives and nullable characteristics.

One original work, storage, node and reference allowance covers opted-in selection, preflight, composition, relationship lookup and dependency scope. A collection summary survives semantic retention failure and presentation omission; exactly one inspection unit is counted. A request-specific 1024-byte mandatory report reserve covers direct fixed-field emission with no summary JSON map. Seven exact Counts, bounded printable reasons and one first-failure suffix omission fit a 768-byte wire bound and a 512-byte early human prefix. The incremental borrowed diagnostic reason ownership is bounded by 272 bytes within the reserved 4096-byte ownership share; optional owner DTOs and location omission reasons have separate precharges. Required relationship inspection examines Model definitions and rejects a Sample root before selection in both views, following ADR 0025: Selected numeric samples. Ordinary raw source inspection of a Sample remains available. Sample sources neither contribute declaration identities nor gain model meaning from SDF-shaped JSON.

## Considered Options

Composing every referenced target would introduce hidden capability work and make a known relationship depend on unused descendant failures. Reconstructing declaration evidence from final content origins loses contributions overwritten during ancestor refinement. Omitting an exhausted or completed-empty collection would make absence indistinguishable from unavailable traversal. A general summary JSON map or redundant wire request flag would add avoidable backend capacity and cloning obligations.
