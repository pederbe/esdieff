## Considered Options

A symbol whitelist would reject permitted URI names and turn dated misses into false current-negative registration claims. Normalizing every URN would conflate represented-index interpretation with identity and hide the namespace case conflict. An arbitrary caller snapshot would broaden ownership, update and trust contracts without the need served by explicit withholding. Per-name mandatory summaries would amplify storage; one fixed owner retains trustworthy aggregate observations while located findings and replacements remain optional. Smaller admission tied to renderer placement would couple semantic truth to fallback lifetimes; two complete extension DTO generations keep the independently charged mandatory boundary stable.

One combined index/component diagnosis would give slash-encoding instructions for a correct index with a malformed suffix. Treating the ordinary question-mark recommendation as a mandatory component error would also hide its SHOULD qualification; separate grammar errors and delimiter advice preserve those bases.
